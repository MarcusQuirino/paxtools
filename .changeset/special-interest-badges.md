Insígnias de interesse especial (special interest badges): tapping an insígnia in a bloco opens its requirement checklist, grouped like the official UEB list. Groups can ask for only some items ("pelo menos duas", "sendo obrigatória a primeira", "Opção 1 ou 2"). Once every group is satisfied the insígnia is earned and substitutes the bloco's ações variáveis. Requirements go through the escotista's Pendentes queue.
